Godrej Florenne · Off Soukya Road, Whitefield

Godrej Florenne is a premium residential address by Godrej Properties, set across 20 acres Off Soukya Road, Whitefield. Designed by celebrated architect Hafeez Contractor, the project draws inspiration from the grandeur of French Renaissance architecture and traditional châteaux that evoke old-world European charm. Spacious 4 & 5 BHK row villas are crafted for gracious living, with light-filled interiors, private balconies and grand terraces.

The project offers seamless connectivity to Sarjapur, Whitefield, KR Puram, Hope Farm Junction and STRR, with reputed schools, hospitals and everyday conveniences close at hand. A touch of France, a whole lot of home. Come to a place where life feels easy, warm and wonderfully yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Godrej Florenne?

Godrej Florenne is a premium community of 4 & 5 BHK row villas by Godrej Properties, set across approximately 20 acres off Soukya Road, Whitefield, East Bengaluru. It is designed by architect Hafeez Contractor and inspired by French Renaissance architecture.

Where is Godrej Florenne located?

The project is at Hemmandahalli, off Soukya Road, Whitefield, Bengaluru. Soukya Road is about 8 minutes away, Whitefield Railway Station about 15 minutes and Hope Farm Junction about 20 minutes by road*.

Which row villas are available, and in what sizes?

Four configurations: 4 BHK Optima (3,700 sq. ft.), 4 BHK Premia (4,000 sq. ft.), 4 BHK Luxe (4,500 sq. ft.) and 5 BHK Luxe (5,500 sq. ft.).

What is the price of Godrej Florenne row villas?

Row villas start from ₹ 5.40 Cr* for the 4 BHK Optima and go up to ₹ 8.00 Cr* onwards for the 5 BHK Luxe. Prices are indicative and subject to change; the sales team shares the complete costing for the row villa you choose.

Is Godrej Florenne RERA registered?

Yes. The project's RERA registration number is PRM/KA/RERA/1250/304/PR/150926/008943.

What does every row villa come with?

Every home has a private garden, a rooftop and a lift, along with private balconies and terraces and light-filled interiors.

What amenities does the community offer?

A grand clubhouse, swimming pool, amphitheatre, gymnasium, sports courts, children's play areas, walking and jogging tracks, landscaped gardens and pet-friendly spaces, with rainwater harvesting and EV charging.

How can I see the master plan and floor plans?

Share your details through any form on this page. The master plan opens right after you submit, and the sales team sends the floor plans and costing for your preferred configuration.

Is this the official Godrej Properties website?

No. This website belongs to an authorised channel partner, Buildnest Realty Pvt Ltd, and is for information purposes only.
