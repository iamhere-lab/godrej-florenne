    About Godrej Florenne

    Godrej Florenne is a premium residential address by Godrej Properties, set across 20 acres Off Soukya Road, Whitefield. Designed by celebrated architect Hafeez Contractor, the project draws inspiration from the grandeur of French Renaissance architecture and traditional châteaux that evoke old-world European charm. Spacious 4 & 5 BHK row houses are crafted for gracious living, with light-filled interiors, private balconies and grand terraces.

    The project offers seamless connectivity to Sarjapur, Whitefield, KR Puram, Hope Farm Junction and STRR, with reputed schools, hospitals and everyday conveniences close at hand. A touch of France, a whole lot of home. Come to a place where life feels easy, warm and wonderfully yours.
